HALL PASS / TEACHER INSTALLATION

Your classroom. Your kiosk.

Set up a private Hall Pass app for a Chromebook by your door—without owning a website.

Development preview, not a production installer. Schoolwide inactive roster synchronization and automatic lunch-number PIN provisioning are not yet enabled. Do not use this preview to migrate real student credentials.

1. Make a private copy

Sign into your school Google account. Make your own copy of the existing Hall Pass template.

Copy Hall Pass template

2. Configure your classroom

In your new spreadsheet select Hall Pass → Start setup. Enter your name, class schedule, school student-email domain and pass rules. Grant Google's requested permissions only from your school account.

3. Activate the web app

Follow the built-in setup wizard. In Google's deploy screen choose Web app, Execute as Me and Who has access: Anyone within your school, if permitted. Copy the new /exec link, not /dev.

A school administrator may need to approve Apps Script and Chromebook kiosk use.

4. Get your two links

This page makes kiosk/teacher URLs locally and does not upload or save the address.

5. Check it with synthetic students

On the dedicated Chromebook, open the kiosk link in fullscreen. Test a request and a return with synthetic data, then confirm the screen clears between students and the teacher dashboard remains protected.

Do not sign a teacher account into the student Chromebook. District IT handles managed kiosk policy and screen-sleep settings.

This ZIP contains no school roster, lunch PINs, student data, Google credentials or tokens.
