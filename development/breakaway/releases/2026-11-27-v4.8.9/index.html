PLAY LIKE A RUNNING BACK.

Near-miss tackles trigger a one-second BREAK FREE challenge: mash E/Space, gamepad A, or the flashing on-field prompt. Clean tackles still bring you down. Touchdowns and tackles trigger a replay that pauses the game clock; skip instantly or switch replays off in Pause.

Use space, timing, and changing direction to make a defender miss. Any defender knocked down by a move, block or missed dive stays down until the next possession. Move forward: if you stall for three seconds, three reserve defenders charge from behind the far end zone.

Score 3 points per new yard, 75 per successful counter, 35 per clean cut, and 600 per touchdown. A block can open a seal burst: gain 10 yards within 1.45 seconds for 40 extra points, once per possession. Each touchdown adds up to two seconds; each possession reset costs a fixed 1.26 seconds, whether you skip the transition or not. The result shows every point.

You begin each game with five blockers. Every touchdown removes one on the NEXT possession until one remains. Tackles and yardage points do not remove them. Fallen defenders stay down for that possession. ON TOUCH: drag the left stick and hold Sprint. After exhaustion, release Sprint once the meter says RELEASE SPRINT, then press it again. Tap the four move buttons on the right. Gamepad: left stick or D-pad to move; A juke, B spin, X stiff, Y hurdle, RT sprint, Start pause. In menus use D-pad and A to select, B to go back. Press M to toggle sound, or use the Sound Effects button when paused. A disconnected controller pauses the drive safely. Press P or Escape to pause on keyboard. Read the defense: outward brackets mark edge contain, inward chevrons mark pressure, double arcs mark the safety. Dotted aqua lines show distinct blocker assignments, not guaranteed blocks. SEALED means good leverage won contact; BEATEN means the blocker was caught out of position. Defenders contain the edge, fill the middle, scrape across, or hold safety depth from sampled reads; they cannot predict future moves. A WINDUP or DIVE cue marks a real committed tackler, and fallen defenders never threaten again. The pause menu offers High Visibility and Reduced Effects settings. Dynamic visual action is not fully playable by screen reader.
