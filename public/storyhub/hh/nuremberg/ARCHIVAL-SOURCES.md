The authoritative hosts initially returned rate-limiting responses to scripted downloads from the authorized workstation. Both JPEG derivatives were retrieved through `images.weserv.nl`, a public image-delivery service using the official source URLs. Both downloaded files were visually inspected against their archival source images, but **their bytes should not be represented as certified archival originals**. The files are now stored and served locally under this folder, with **no required cross-origin image requests** during classroom use.

**Do not** claim either photograph depicts the moment of execution. They show the courtroom and Woods, respectively. The portrait is intentionally kept at approximately its small native dimensions, not stretched into a fake large portrait.
