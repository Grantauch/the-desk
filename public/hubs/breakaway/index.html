PLAY LIKE A RUNNING BACK.

Use space, timing, and changing direction to make a defender miss. Any defender knocked down by a move, block or missed dive stays down until the next possession. Move forward: if you stall for three seconds, three reserve defenders charge from behind the far end zone.

ON TOUCH: drag the left stick and hold Sprint. Tap the four move buttons on the right. Gamepad: left stick or D-pad to move; A juke, B spin, X stiff, Y hurdle, RT sprint, Start pause. In menus use D-pad and A to select, B to go back. Press M to toggle sound, or use the Sound Effects button when paused. A disconnected controller pauses the drive safely. Press P or Escape to pause on keyboard. The pause menu offers High Visibility and Reduced Effects settings. Dynamic visual action is not fully playable by screen reader.
