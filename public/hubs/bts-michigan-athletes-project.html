      Help

      What do I do next?

      Press the gold What do I do next? button at the top. It jumps to your next empty box and lights it up.

      How long does a box need to be?

      Two or three sentences. The box turns green and says Done when it is long enough. Fact boxes only need a short sentence.

      Where do I find the answers?

      On your athlete's page. Press Open the athlete page at the top. You do not need any other website.

      Why can I not type in some boxes?

      Those boxes belong to your partner. You can read what they wrote, but only they can change it. Your grade comes from your own boxes.

      My partner is absent

      Keep going on your own jobs. Their boxes will wait for them. If they are gone tomorrow too, tell Mr. Auch.

      Can I paste?

      Short quotes of 12 words or fewer, yes. Longer pastes are blocked, and the page keeps a timeline of how answers were written. Write it the way you would say it.

      Did my work save?

      Yes. It saves to your account by itself every few seconds. You can sign in on any Chromebook and pick up where you left off.

      How much time do we have?

      Two days. Finish the fact file and chapter one today. Finish chapter two, your reflection and Turn In tomorrow.

      We want a different athlete

      Press Change athlete at the top. You can only pick someone no other team in your class has.

      I picked the wrong thing during setup

      If no partner has joined yet, press Start over at the top. If your team is already full, ask Mr. Auch.
