01 / The place behind the punches

BEFORE THE
BELL RANG.

Micky Ward didn't grow up in a sports-movie set. He grew up in Lowell, Massachusetts. A city that had once employed thousands making cloth was still figuring out what came next.

LOWELL / INDUSTRIAL BACKDROP
1890s

Illustration is a teaching diagram, not a historical photograph.

A CITY BUILT ON WORK

A CITY OF MILLS. A CITY OF DREAMS.

Lowell's textile factories drew generations of workers, including Irish and other immigrant families. Opportunity often came with long hours, dangerous machinery, and unequal power.

A place can change industries in a generation. Families live with the consequences for decades.

The 1890s mark a period of significant mill employment, not Lowell's founding.

WHILE YOU WATCH: Micky works outside the ring, paving roads. Look for how having to earn a living affects his freedom to train, turn down fights, or leave home. Who in the movie has the luxury of making mistakes?

04 / The judge's desk

THE CROWD
ISN'T THE JUDGE.

A roar isn't a score. You get three imaginary rounds. Decide who won each one — then compare your scorecard to a reasoned example.

ROUND 1 · THE BUSY FIGHTER

Blue throws 32 punches but most hit gloves. Red lands 12 clean shots and controls the distance. The crowd cheers Blue.

ROUND 2 · ONE BIG MOMENT

Blue boxes well early. Red lands a clean punch that knocks Blue down once. Blue gets up, and the round ends with no other major incident.

ROUND 3 · THE LATE SURGE

Red wins the first half with clean jabs. Blue finishes strongly, lands more clean punches overall, and cuts off the ring.

REAL RULE: Judges consider clean punching, effective aggression, ring generalship, and defense. In a 10-point-must system, the round winner usually gets 10.

06 / Life after the final scene

THE CREDITS
ARE NOT THE END.

The final scene of a sports movie can feel like the end of the story. It usually isn't. Micky had three of his most famous fights after the period covered by the film.

Real fighters: Ward & Gatti · photo via Boxing News
THE FIGHT CONTINUED.

Ward’s later career includes a three-fight rivalry with Arturo Gatti, pictured here. This real photograph was taken in 2003. Reveal it after your class finishes the movie.

2002 / IWard defeats Arturo Gatti by majority decision. Their first meeting becomes a modern boxing classic.
2002 / IIGatti wins the rematch by unanimous decision. Now they're tied.
2003 / IIIGatti wins the deciding bout by unanimous decision. Ward retires that year.
