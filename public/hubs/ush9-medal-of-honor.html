How it works

Three things you’ll make

A report

Answer small questions one box at a time. The project page turns your answers into a finished paper for Mr. Auch to print.

A wall poster

Create a hand-drawn poster for the classroom. The project page gives you a blueprint with your headline, facts, and scene.

A memorial screen

Design a digital memorial in the project workspace to display on the Promethean board.

Questions

Do I need to search the internet?

No. Everything you need is on your veteran's page. The links at the bottom of each page are only for students who want to go deeper.

How do I start a team?

Open My project and sign in with your lunch PIN. One partner presses Start a team and picks the veteran, which shows a four letter code. The other partner signs in on their own Chromebook, presses Join my partner and types that code.

Can my partner do my part?

No. Each box belongs to one person, and only that person can type in it. Your grade comes from your own boxes and your own art piece.

My partner is absent. What do I do?

Keep working on your own boxes. Their boxes wait for them. If your partner is gone for several days, tell Mr. Auch.

How long do my answers need to be?

Two or three sentences per box. A box turns green when it is long enough.

Can I copy from the veteran page?

No. Long pastes are blocked and the page keeps a record of how your answers were written. Read the page, then write it the way you would say it.

Does my art have to be good?

It has to be complete and show real effort. Stick figures are fine if the scene, facts and quote are all there.

Can we change veterans?

Yes, on the first day, if another team in your class has not already taken the one you want.

Stories are written for this class from the Wikipedia articles and official records linked on each veteran's page. Citations are quoted word for word from the Army and Navy records. Medal totals from the Pritzker Military Museum & Library.
