Follow the record

Corrected student edition. The document excerpts are primary sources; the sorting tasks and scenarios are classroom practice, not a test or a grade. Legal rights and lived conditions did not change at the same pace.

  • National Archives: the 1787 Constitution — the three passages in section two.
  • National Archives: Amendments 13, 14 and 15 — abolition and its punishment exception, citizenship and equal protection, and the ban on racial discrimination in voting.
  • U.S. Senate: Enforcement Acts, 1870–1871 — federal action against political violence, not a guarantee that violence ended.
  • National Archives: land promises and the Southern Homestead Act — land laws existed, but there was no general guarantee of land for every freed family.
  • National Park Service: Reconstruction and its retreat — rights, resistance, labor and the limits of enforcement.
