FIELD MANUAL / 01

THE PLAYBOOK.

Full Drive is 100 yards; Score Attack is a 50-yard sprint for bronze, silver, or gold. Read each defensive formation, change lanes, hurdle or stiff-arm to finish.

01 / VISION

READ THE GAP.

Orange lanes are covered. The green lane is your best opening. Read-blitz defenders may shift early, but they always commit before the contact window.

02 / FOOTWORK

MAKE THE CUT.

Use ←→ or AD to switch lanes. On phones, tap the arrows or swipe sideways.

03 / FINISH

BEAT THE BLITZ.

SPACE to hurdle. F to stiff-arm. Swipe up or down on the field for either move.
