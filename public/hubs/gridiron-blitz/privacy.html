GRIDIRON BLITZ / FREE GRANTDESK GAME

Privacy & fair play.

No account. No leaderboard. No tracking endpoint.

This free, noncommercial GrantDesk game runs in your browser and stores career progress on this device. It does not transmit your game scores, your name, a school PIN, or gameplay events to a Gridiron Blitz server. There is no in-game account or purchase flow.

What stays on your device?

Your career, game records, unlocked uniforms, coins, XP, settings and any earlier Gridiron Blitz saves are kept in browser local storage under Gridiron Blitz-specific keys. The game reads legacy saves to protect previous progress. Clearing website storage or using a different browser/device may erase or hide your local progress.

Are public scores verified?

No. Local scores can be edited using browser developer tools, so we do not publish rankings, award real-world prizes, or imply that a local high score is verified. Any future leaderboard needs a server-validated play/replay protocol and anti-abuse safeguards before activation.

Does this page collect analytics?

The B09 game integration has no analytics calls, advertising SDKs or third-party scripts. The surrounding website and hosting provider may separately process standard request logs under their own policies; this page does not make claims about those systems.

Does the game charge money?

No. Coins and uniforms are earned in play. There are no purchases, loot boxes, gambling, or marketing offers in this build.

Accessibility and feedback

You can use keyboard, touchscreen, or a compatible gamepad, and change graphics, audio and reduced-motion preferences in game settings. Hardware accessibility and motion-comfort reviews are still scheduled before the final commercial launch.

Support and troubleshooting

If the field does not load, update your browser and try the lower graphics setting. If your progress is missing, check whether you are using the same browser, device and website origin. To report a problem, use the contact information on the GrantDesk About page. Please do not send school PINs or passwords with game feedback.
