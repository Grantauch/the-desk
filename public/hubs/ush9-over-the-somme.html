The first day was only the beginning. When the offensive was called off in November, the British and French had pushed the line forward about six miles. More than a million men on all sides had been killed or wounded.

Picardy, France · Western Front

Over the Somme

For seven days British guns fired about 1.5 million shells at the German line. Commanders expected the infantry to walk across a dead front. At 7:30 on the morning of 1 July 1916 they climbed out of their trenches and found the wire uncut and the machine gunners alive in deep chalk dugouts.

    1. Fly low along the German front line. The week of shelling was supposed to destroy it. What about the ground there let so many defenders survive?
