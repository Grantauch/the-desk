    Why America waited

    In August 1914 President Woodrow Wilson told Americans to stay neutral. Millions of them had parents born in Germany, Ireland or Britain, and most wanted no part of a European fight. American banks and factories kept selling to both sides, though far more to Britain and France, because the British navy blocked German ports.

    • May 7, 1915

      A German submarine sinks the British liner Lusitania off Ireland. 1,198 people die, 128 of them Americans. Germany later promises to warn ships before sinking them.

    • Feb 1, 1917

      Germany breaks that promise and declares unrestricted submarine warfare. Its generals bet they can starve Britain before American troops arrive.

    • Mar 1, 1917

      American newspapers print the Zimmermann Telegram. Germany had offered Mexico the return of Texas, New Mexico and Arizona if Mexico would attack the United States.

    • Apr 2, 1917

      Wilson asks Congress for war and tells it that "the world must be made safe for democracy."
