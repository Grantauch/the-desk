        <h1>Then the owners locked down the players</h1>
        <p class="lead">Owners had another problem: players could create bidding wars by moving to whoever paid more.</p>
        <p class="bodycopy">The reserve system changed that. A club could list certain players as “reserved,” and the other clubs agreed not to negotiate with them. If nobody else is allowed to bid, the player loses most of his leverage.</p>
