      Help

      What do I do next?

      Press the gold What do I do next? button at the top. It jumps to your next empty box and lights it up.

      How long does a box need to be?

      Two or three sentences. The box turns green and says Done when it is long enough. Fact and word boxes only need a short sentence.

      Where do I find the answers?

      On your veteran's page. Press Open his page at the top. You do not need any other website.

      Why can I not type in some boxes?

      Those boxes belong to your partner. You can read what they wrote, but only they can change it. Your grade comes from your own boxes.

      My partner is absent

      Keep going on your own jobs. Their boxes will wait for them. If they are gone for a few days, tell Mr. Auch.

      Can I paste?

      Short quotes of 12 words or fewer, yes. Longer pastes are blocked, and the page keeps a timeline of how answers were written. Write it the way you would say it.

      Did my work save?

      Yes. It saves to your account by itself every few seconds. The strip under the top bar shows when it last saved. You can sign in on any Chromebook and pick up where you left off.

      What is the poster?

      Partner A draws a poster by hand for the classroom wall. Fill in the poster boxes in Step 6, print the blueprint, and copy it onto the poster paper.

      What is the memorial screen?

      Partner B designs a picture in Step 6 right on this page. It shows on the Promethean board and becomes the cover of your printed paper.

      We want a different veteran

      Press Change veteran at the top. You can only pick someone no other team in your class has.

      I picked the wrong thing during setup

      If no partner has joined yet, press Start over at the top. If your team is already full, ask Mr. Auch.
