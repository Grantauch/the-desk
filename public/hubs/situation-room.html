Timeline and source documents

October 16 · The photographs

Photographs taken October 14 revealed missile site construction. Kennedy was briefed on October 16 and consulted a small group of advisers before announcing the discovery on October 22.

National Archives · October 14 aerial photograph

October 22 · The quarantine

Kennedy announced a naval quarantine on offensive military shipments to Cuba. His televised address explained the scope of the measure and called for the missiles to be withdrawn.

JFK Library · Kennedy’s October 22 address

October 25 · The tanker

The United States allowed the Soviet tanker Bucharest to pass. The JFK Library timeline describes its cargo as petroleum products.

JFK Library · October 25 timeline and meeting records

October 27 · The shootdown

A U-2 was shot down over Cuba, killing Major Rudolf Anderson Jr. Kennedy resisted calls for immediate retaliation while the administration continued to seek an agreement.

JFK Library · October 27 timeline and meeting records

October 27 · The two letters

The U.S. public reply offered a pledge against invading Cuba in exchange for removal of the Soviet missiles. In a private meeting with Soviet ambassador Anatoly Dobrynin, Robert Kennedy discussed the eventual removal of U.S. missiles from Turkey.

  • Office of the Historian · Kennedy’s public reply
  • Office of the Historian · Robert Kennedy’s account of the meeting
October 28 · The reply

Khrushchev announced that the Soviet weapons would be dismantled and returned. Negotiations over implementation continued, and the U.S. lifted the quarantine on November 20.
