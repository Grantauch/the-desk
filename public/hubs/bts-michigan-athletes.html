Questions

Do I need to search the internet?

No. Everything you need is on your athlete's page. The links at the bottom of each page are only for students who want to go deeper.

How do I start a team?

Open My project and sign in with your lunch PIN. One partner presses Start a team and picks the athlete, which shows a four letter code. The other partner signs in on their own Chromebook, presses Join my partner and types that code.

Can my partner do my part?

No. Each box belongs to one person, and only that person can type in it. Your grade comes from your own boxes.

My partner is absent. What do I do?

Keep working on your own boxes. Their boxes wait for them. If your partner is gone tomorrow too, tell Mr. Auch.

How long do my answers need to be?

Two or three sentences per box. A box turns green when it is long enough.

Can I copy from the athlete page?

No. Long pastes are blocked and the page keeps a record of how your answers were written. Read the page, then write it the way you would say it.

Can we change athletes?

Yes, today, if another team in your class has not already taken the one you want.
