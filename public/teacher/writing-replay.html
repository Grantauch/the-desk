A replay shows how text reached the answer box in one browser tab. It cannot see other devices, other people, or what happened before the page opened. Typed text can still be copied by hand from another screen, and dictation or autocomplete can add text at once. Treat anything here as a reason to ask the student about the work, never as proof by itself.
