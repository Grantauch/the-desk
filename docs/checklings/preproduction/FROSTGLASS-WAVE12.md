# Frostglass Reach — arctic creature and exploration brief (candidate Wave Twelve)

**Proposal only.** Corresponds to register slots **CK133–CK144**, Wave 12. Creature names below are provisional, not legally/originality cleared, not playable species, and not an approval to add mechanics, health rules or art.

## Arctic identity

Frostglass is not just a snow-colored forest. Its enormous views across snowfields contrast with tight ice caves, stone shelters, frozen basins and blue meltwater channels. Geography should be readable at a glance: darker safe gravel paths, high-contrast cairns, clearly marked return tracks and distinctive blue ice formations. Some slippery-looking routes can be scenic without introducing involuntary movement, unwarned fatalities or paywalled equipment.

**Four authored anchors:** Blueglass Lake, North Cairn, Rime Shelter, Wind Carved Arch. Optional exploration loops connect small hidden caves, meltwater pools and low ridges. The default entrance must remain a safe and reliable path back to the Wilds even when the map has scenery variation. Snowfall may change presentation; it must not reroll the permanent landscape, force health decay on an absent student or block school-device access.

## Design pitfalls to review

- **Rimesip versus Quarzell:** both seals must be immediately distinguished (sleek water crest and icicle whiskers versus broad rocky body and twin quartz tusks) or one concept should be redesigned.
- **Sliddew versus Skidril (Bramblefen):** both involve skating; distinguish arctic blade-feet, body silhouette, temperament and encounter environment clearly.
- **Granmusk versus Chertail:** stone-armored/moss-overlap risk; avoid generic gray rock silhouettes.
- **Cold should not become an unapproved ninth battle type.** Tide/Stone are ecological strengths here, and existing Storm, Meadow, Shade or other types may still appear as the wider roster evolves.
- **Snow, storms and outages must not kill absent Checklings.** The existing body clock and intentionally chosen rare-risk mechanics govern death; this biome does not introduce invisible environmental perma-death.
- Preserve authored paths, in-progress saves, teacher pause, anonymous identity and limited phrase chat. No free text, breeding, shiny variants, held items, paid loot or compulsory daily long expeditions.

## First playable arctic slice

After the shared-world and Chromebook gates pass, build a **256×256 Frostglass proof** with Blueglass Lake, North Cairn and one ice cave, plus two reliably returnable routes. Use a tiny approved species subset (e.g. one Tide and one Stone) and enough safe habitats for a newer trainer. Ensure ice and snow tile readability in reduced motion and on low-resolution Chromebooks. Progress should save by location and the ordinary trainer/Checkling persistence rules, not fragile client-only flags.
