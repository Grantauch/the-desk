# Bramblefen Basin — candidate Wave Two (CK013–CK024)

**Preproduction creative brief; unapproved candidates.** Works under `DESIGN.md`, locked `wave-one.md` and the new 12-biome plan. None of these names, stats, move effects, evolution forms, habitats or art exists in the live game. Claude/Grant must approve or rewrite before canonical naming and implementation. All four founder lines and original Bramble/Mossbit/Reedling remain untouched.

## The region's identity

Bramblefen is a **wetland, not another ordinary forest**. Footpaths follow raised peat ridges; the safest roads end at reed-choked pools. The water is shallow but unpredictable. Giant willow roots, cattail groves, submerged fallen logs and amber moss create recognizable silhouettes, while waterways open alternate routes for exploration. All the following are *design intentions*, not a finished map.

### Early landmark candidates

| Place | Recognition | Gameplay purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Crooked Footbridge | Bent timbers crossing the broad slow river | Safe entry, return route and first low-level creatures |
| Reed Choir | Tall cattails whose seed pods click in the wind | Sound-led discovery and Tide habitat |
| The Sleeping Willow | One immense willow with a dry root chamber | Shelter, character encounter and early lore |
| Pollen Flats | Bright flowers on a high bank, airborne seed tufts | Meadow creatures and flying-seed motif |
| Glasswater Pool | Unnaturally still pond reflecting hidden things | Later discovery and reflective-shell creatures |
| Mudtrumpet Hollow | Hollow reeds near a quiet sink | Mid-level route and sound pattern objective |
| Twin Current Fork | Two streams rejoining below a fallen log | Route choice with guaranteed return path |
| Drowned Steps | Stone stairs that disappear beneath peat water | Longer-term authored mystery and high-level encounters |

Procedural wetlands must never submerge the sole safe exit, block a student from returning home, or corrupt class-specific progress. A real map requires multiple connected paths and approved encounter data.

## Proposed acquisition and release cadence

- **Early shared-wilderness encounters:** CK013, CK015, CK021, CK023, at levels compatible with newly arrived trainers after the class gate. Calm wetland edge and footbridge should be safe from high-level encounters.
- **Deep-water and hidden bank encounters:** CK016, CK019, CK024, with alternate routes and clear return paths.
- **Patience/observation encounters:** CK017, CK018, CK020, CK022. Encounters can depend on discovering appropriate *locations*, not a newly invented day/night cycle.
- **Multiple paths:** wild catches and a few quest-mediated first discoveries are reasonable; do not introduce paid eggs, opaque random boxes, breeding or trading.
- **The existing 12-line roster remains usable throughout.** No new species can dominate solely because it lives deeper in the biome.

## Balance work before an art commission

For each candidate line, Claude and the art lead should approve its silhouette and personality, then specify base HP/Power/Stamina/Speed, per-level growth, four legal moves (one free) and its type. Proposed signatures should be expressible by **current supported move components**—damage, guard, stamina restore, shields, phases and approved counter bonuses—unless a separately reviewed engine enhancement is necessary.

For example, *Thornlock* can become a Warder through a shield-bearing move; it does not need an unapproved passive ability. *Mudhorn* can feel like a heavy Artillery creature via a costly move with a clear wind-up rhythm; it should not simply have the highest raw Power. Prototype each entry against the exact current four founders and future approved Wave One species using the isolated real-engine battle lab.

**Approval gates:** readable 32px silhouette → original name/collision review → written care and habitat behavior → legal stats and move data → deterministic matchup simulation → four final required art frames → real Chromebook playtest → release. This document clears none of those gates by itself.
