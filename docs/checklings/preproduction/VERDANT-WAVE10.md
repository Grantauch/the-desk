# Verdant Crown — jungle creature and exploration brief (candidate Wave Ten)

**Proposal only.** Corresponds to register slots **CK109–CK120**, Wave 10. None is an approved original name, implemented battle species, finished evolution, or commissioned art. Claude and Grant review silhouette, naming, care history and combat prior to release.

## Jungle identity

Verdant Crown is a living maze of **three elevations**: the forest floor (cool root caves and muddy pools), middle canopy (walkable root bridges and hanging vines) and sunlit crown (orchids and bright clearings). These are navigational layers/connected maps if needed, **not a new battle type or unapproved flying mechanic**. The terrain generator owns undergrowth variation; authored landmarks and routes must remain permanent under the same seed. A 3–5 minute return trip should yield worthwhile discoveries near the entrance; deep expedition paths can take much longer.

**Four authored anchors:** Crownfall Waterfall, Vine Court, Old Canopy Bridge, Temple Root. Supporting safe routes lead to a return gate; foliage density cannot obscure the only exit. The waterfall includes a genuine, visible water barrier and a maintained bridge, not a single decorative blue stripe. The temple should be a handcrafted narrative landmark, not procedural corridor spam.

## Protected gameplay boundaries

- The first 12 Checklings remain unchanged; new jungle species are **not** more powerful just because Verdant Crown is a late-region biome.
- Care-morph candidates must have three genuinely viable care-history outcomes at the approved level threshold. The neglected outcome can be scrappy and competitive but repeated neglect still carries condition, illness and death risks. No care outcome should reward intentionally endangering a pet as an always-optimal strategy.
- Avoid new **passive abilities, free-text chat, day/night-cycle gating, held items, breeding, double battles or paid boxes**. These are outside the current Design Bible.
- No species should be captured from landscape decoration alone. Server-authoritative tall-grass habitat records, bounded catch chances and anti-duplicate rewards remain necessary.
- Simple jungle environmental storytelling (fallen fruit, twisting roots, washed-out paths, canopy noise) is valuable; permanent geographical landmarks must not reroll per student.
- Morphology overlaps to review: Petalisk and Veilmoth both orchid-inspired but must be distinguishable as mantis versus moth; Pouchbat must not resemble the existing Zee or Cloudlet; Cavemur and Loopari need unrelated facial shapes and movement.

**First build slice (after architecture acceptance):** a 256×256 prototype jungle region with one permanent bridge, Crownfall Waterfall, two contrasting habitats, an always-open return route, and only 2–4 approved creatures to test actual exploration—not all twelve at once.
