# Kettle Creek fixes · November 2, 2026

Owner: Claude. Built on the resident lives release (0.15.0), from Grant's review of a Kettle Creek screenshot.

**Fences and the gate.** The practice yard and the cemetery fences are drawn tile by tile from a fence kit cut from the approved fence art. The sides now run north and south like fences seen from above, the corners join, and the practice yard has a two leaf gate that swings open when anyone walks up to it. Three practice stumps stand inside, as the yard's sign always promised.

**Paths.** Lanes are autotiled at the art's pixel size, with wandering edges, grass tufts, a dark lip under the grass and pebbles. This replaces the flat rounded lanes. Door lanes run straight with no stair steps, a lane that ended at the cemetery fence was cut back, and benches and the bus stop sit on dirt pads. A Gemini request for a hand drawn path tileset is in `docs/checklings/art-requests/2026-11-02-path-tileset.md`.

**Flowers.** Flower patches are small pixel flowers instead of outlined squares, and none sit in the middle of a lane.

**The south lane.** The wall of trees between Kettle Creek and the resident south lane, with its one tile gap where trainers got stuck in the hedge, is open meadow now. The same is true in the town. Roofs are solid in Kettle Creek and on the south lanes, so nobody can walk behind a house and disappear.

**Checklings in the world.** Every trainer's partner Checkling walks one tile behind them along their exact path.

**The screen.** In Kettle Creek the chip reads Kettle Creek and "Your home town · only you are here". It no longer shows "Checkaverse Kettle Creek" or a live count that always said one. The first ride's welcome says Kettle Creek. Long trainer names shorten instead of running off screen. Residents always show a name tag nearby, and a tap goes to the resident nearest the finger. The map is drawn at the screen's real pixels, so the art stays sharp.

**Not changed.** Server rules, care, battles, residents' lives, saves and school services are unchanged. Only the maps' solid tiles, the client drawing and two sign lines changed. Rowan's ribbon moved one tile east, off the new cottage roof row.

Class check: walk into the practice yard through the gate, walk behind every building, cross into the south lane, and watch a partner follow around corners.
