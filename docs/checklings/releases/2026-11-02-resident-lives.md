# Resident lives · 0.15.0

Owner: Codex. Core loop step 4 in the existing Checkaverse.

All twelve neighbors have visitable homes and real work, travel, visits and rest. Pax carries parcels to the clinic, Nell receives them, gardens are watered, paths are swept and neighbors meet or leave notes. Their dialogue and history reflect these actions. Around town can find a resident wherever they currently are, including another map or home.

The town and wilds share one server simulation. Each saved trainer's Kettle Creek has private resident history, recovered across reconnects and server restarts. Returned story objects enter the next relevant activity. Teacher pause freezes resident time; quiet scenery keeps the shared positions current with less animation.

Class check: follow a parcel from the store to the clinic, visit a home while its owner is out and after they return, ask about completed work, find a field worker from town, then try teacher pause and quiet scenery. Original player plots, school services, pet care and battle rewards stay compatible. School Chromebook/classroom acceptance remains pending.
