# Checkaverse 0.16.0: Sprite Book Waves 5 to 7

What changed for students:

- 60 new Checklings. Sixteen new lines show up in the wilds and on Route 1, and the rest are what they grow into.
- Checklings evolve. Founders and the two stage lines change at levels 15 and 30. Care lines change once at level 20, and how the Checkling was kept picks the form.
- Caught and egg Checklings can be named from the partner menu.
- Every battle shows smooth, full-size art, the founders included.
- One check-in now outweighs a full day of battling (battle XP stops at 90 a day; a check-in is 100).
- Type advantage is gentler (1.15 strong, 0.85 weak), so no matchup between forms at the same stage is more lopsided than 9 in 10.

What to test in class: ride the bus with a student near level 15, catch something on Route 1, name it, and battle with it.

Art: 61 sheets from the Sprite Book Wave 5, 6 and 7 folders passed `tools/admit-checklings.py` and the hand review in `content/checkling-art-review.json`. Seventeen sheets were drawn as front, action, hurt and back instead of the standard four poses. They use the action pose in the idle, and the hurt pose is saved as `hurt-<id>.png` for later. Drive has duplicate Wave 6 and Wave 7 folders; the newest copy of each sheet was used.
