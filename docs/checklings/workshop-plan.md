# Workshop build plans - Pixel Camp 6.3

The eight existing keepsake recipes show actual supplies beside the required quantities, a labeled native progress bar, and the exact amount still to gather. A recipe with missing supplies offers **Plan supplies**. Its trail plan stays visible while gathering, leads to the next required resource, and points an exhausted friend to free rest. Gathering still costs the existing 15 energy and adds up to three supplies. The plan never grants resources or builds automatically.

When all supplies are available, **Open workshop** focuses that recipe's Build button. Building uses the existing engine's cost, unlock and duplicate guards. Re-placing a built keepsake stays free. Students can change a plan, return to its recipe or put it away without spending supplies. Locked special keepsakes lead to their founder's existing journey. Completed journey cards now say when their keepsake is already built and open that exact recipe.

The plan ID lives only in browser memory. It introduces no save field, private command, recipe, art, species or engine rule. Reloading starts with no active plan while retaining all earned materials and keepsakes. Existing school save formats 1 and 2 keep their earlier capabilities; the workshop and plan require format 3. Reading recipe quantities or choosing a plan at the current stop creates no extra school save. Normal gathering, travel and building use the existing school save path. Official identity, attendance, points and passes remain separate.

An unfinished spar takes priority over starting a supply trip. The existing combatants, health, stamina and turn are preserved, with keyboard focus on the leave control. Original founder images and the verified Version 46 private school source are unchanged. The preceding 6.2 release was merged as `51edfc450c0d3ea4a6a979fd8e60fe5c61892bc3`, passed protected and post-merge checks, and was verified on both public websites.

Eight synthetic browser journeys cover actual gather-to-build play, exact costs and duplicate placement, exports and reloads, partial/surplus supplies, exhausted free rest, changing/canceling plans, locked and built story keepsakes, unfinished spar preservation, 320/390/1366 layouts with automated WCAG A/AA, school formats 1/2/3, unchanged official nickname, absence of attendance/pass writes and shared-device clearing. These are separate from protected CI, deployment, independent peer review and a real classroom/physical Chromebook cycle. The first-twelve admission gates and broader species expansion remain unapproved.
