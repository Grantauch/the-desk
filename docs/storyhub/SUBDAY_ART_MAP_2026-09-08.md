## Delivery rules

- Every image has explicit dimensions to prevent layout shift.
- Only hero art loads eagerly; all narrative art is lazy-loaded and asynchronously decoded.
- Captions identify the images as generated visual reconstructions.
- Dense imagery remains separate from lesson copy; no body text is placed over busy art.
- Mobile layouts stack the image above its caption and remove decorative rotation.
- Reduced-motion layouts retain a readable static image and remove decorative transforms.
