# Blacktop Kings choice artwork v4

Ten original images replace the four mode illustrations and five player-style illustrations and add a separate empty locker room for the animated creator preview. The built-in image generator used the earlier menu screenshot as a palette and illustration reference. The images retain warm brick, gold light, navy shadows, black contours and cel shading. They use different fictional casts, builds, hair, clothing, locations and actions, including white, Black, Latino, East Asian and South Asian players. These are AI-generated illustrations with an inked graphic-novel treatment.

The Career tile now uses its complete illustration with the existing portrait as its loading/error fallback. Creator and Locker previews load the separate player-free background. Player-style cards contain the whole action image within their existing 112-pixel panels, avoiding lost hands, balls and hoops on wider cards. Mode images crop from the top to retain faces in shallow phone cards. Existing labels, card sizes, navigation, customization, game mechanics, save format and soundtrack are retained. Earlier asset files remain available in their versioned directories.
