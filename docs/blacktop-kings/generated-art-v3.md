# Blacktop Kings court details v3

Eleven additional original images were created with the built-in image generator using the approved Lincoln Lot painting and Slasher illustration as style references. The batch continues the warm golden light, thick ink contours, angular cel shading and charcoal shadows of the first two editions. Original PNGs and complete prompts are preserved in an additive review package outside the public repository. The optimized images add 548,432 bytes; all three batches contain 34 images totaling 5,275,586 bytes.

| Six fictional neighborhood spectators | Sideline crowd, with existing bounce and excitement movement |
| Bench, bags, cooler and towels | Sideline, in front of the spectators and behind the players |
| Neutral jersey mesh and folds | Existing moving jersey shape, blended with the chosen team color |

Street boards appear at Lincoln Lot, The Cage, The Underpass and Harbor Yard. The Boardwalk, Skyline Roof, The Pit and Crown Court use glass. The existing target square, rim and net remain procedural. Backboard material follows hoop shake and disappears when the board shatters; padding remains. The transparent spectators retain the existing placement and varied crowd heights. Night courts cache a darker sprite treatment once per source image. Jersey material is clipped to the animated torso; trim and numbers are painted afterward so customization and readability remain intact.

Each court requests its scenery, floor, board and shared detail images on demand. Requests are deduplicated across courts. Only scenery/floor completions rebuild the static background; dynamic materials are painted during the regular frame. Missing or pending images retain the original canvas drawing.
