# Competitive balance lab

PR #177 adds a repeatable real-engine balance gate to the normal release verification. The default run completes 600 seeded games: 288 mirrored Quick Crew games, 160 mirrored archetype games, 120 games across Chill/Street/Legend, and all 32 court/rule combinations. Each mirrored pair starts from its own repeatable seed. The optional deep run expands the sample to 824 games.

Crown Rules now awards at most +6 points and removes at most 2 opponent points for a deep Double Crown basket, an eight-point swing. Standard-rule Crown scoring retains its existing formula. Passing from a strong distributor opens a brief receiver shooting rhythm window; the Floor General rating budget shifts one steal point to outside shooting. Dunk reach is slightly shorter, and high-handle AI players use their handles more often. Pogo gains inside finishing/rebounding and Tie-Dye gains speed/handling to lift the weak Lot Lizards lineup.

The larger sample exposed a weak pure Trickster lineup and too much advantage for Big teams. Final tuning redistributes their existing 36-point budgets: Tricksters gain inside/outside finishing while Big teams trade some finishing, blocks and rebounds for passing/outside shooting. Their speed, handling and body identities remain distinct. The harness also checks every archetype retains exactly 36 base points.

Both final samples passed every original guardrail. The 600-game release sample has Quick Crew wins from 21.9% to 75.0% and archetypes from 31.3% to 71.9%. The 824-game deep sample has Quick Crew wins from 24.0% to 76.0% and archetypes from 31.3% to 70.8%. These are broad regression limits, not a claim that every lineup wins equally often. In the release sample, Quick Crew games averaged 44.9% field goals, 2.06 blocks, 2.81 steals, 0.66 ankle breakers and 5.98 offensive rebounds. Legend produced 0.806 points per attempt versus Chill's 0.588 and finished faster on average.

The report measures deep/inside/layup/dunk scoring, blocks, steals, ankle breakers, offensive rebounds and attempts/makes/field-goal rate for each perfect/slightly early/slightly late/early/late release category. Quick Crew simulated jumpers made 96.8% of perfect releases, 94.1% slightly early, 88.2% slightly late, 6.6% early and 0.7% late. An observed earlier deep-sample 9-10 game scored its winner at 12:05; the deep liveness ceiling allows twenty simulated minutes, while the release gate retains twelve. No win-rate, efficiency or difficulty thresholds were relaxed. Full canonical verification passed, including existing gameplay, poses, art, audio, public resources, 53 built routes, 103 HTML files/4,347 local references and all seven sealed StoryHub releases.
