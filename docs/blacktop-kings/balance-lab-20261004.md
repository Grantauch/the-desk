# Competitive balance lab

PR #177 adds a repeatable real-engine balance gate to the normal release verification. The default run completes 600 seeded games: 288 mirrored Quick Crew games, 160 mirrored archetype games, 120 games across Chill/Street/Legend, and all 32 court/rule combinations. Each mirrored pair starts from its own repeatable seed. The optional deep run expands the sample to 824 games.

Crown Rules now awards at most +6 points and removes at most 2 opponent points for a deep Double Crown basket, an eight-point swing. Standard-rule Crown scoring retains its existing formula. Passing from a strong distributor opens a brief receiver shooting rhythm window; the Floor General rating budget shifts one steal point to outside shooting. Dunk reach is slightly shorter, and high-handle AI players use their handles more often. Pogo gains inside finishing/rebounding and Tie-Dye gains speed/handling to lift the weak Lot Lizards lineup.

The first 600-game release run passed, but the larger sample exposed a weak pure Trickster lineup and too much advantage for Big teams. The final tuning redistributes their existing 36-point budgets: Tricksters gain inside/outside finishing while Big teams trade some finishing, blocks and rebounds for passing/outside shooting. Their speed, handling and body identities remain distinct. Current full release and focused deep archetype checks are in progress; their final results supersede the first sample.

The report measures deep/inside/layup/dunk scoring, blocks, steals, ankle breakers, offensive rebounds and attempts/makes/field-goal rate for each perfect/slightly early/slightly late/early/late release category. An observed deep-sample 9-10 game scored its winner at 12:05; the deep liveness ceiling allows twenty simulated minutes, while the release gate retains twelve. No win-rate, efficiency or difficulty thresholds were relaxed. Current canonical verification includes the existing gameplay, poses, art, audio, public-resource and sealed StoryHub gates.
