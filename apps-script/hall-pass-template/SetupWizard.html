The first time takes about 20 seconds.

Google couldn’t open your setup

  1. Open a Chrome profile signed in only to your school account, or an Incognito window with only that account signed in.
  2. Open your own copy of the Hall Pass spreadsheet. Click the large Start setup / show my links button on the opening page. The Hall Pass menu is a backup. Approve Google’s permissions if asked, then open Start setup again.
  3. If it still fails, ask school IT to check Apps Script access for your account. Give them the Google message below and your spreadsheet link privately.

Keep your existing copy. You do not need to delete tabs or clear settings.

Step 3 of 4

Turn it on

Google needs you to switch it on yourself, once. It is about six clicks.

  1. Open the program ↗
    A new tab opens with the Hall Pass program. You do not need to change anything there.
  2. Top right, click the blue Deploy, then New deployment.
  3. Click the gear ⚙ next to “Select type” and choose Web app.
  4. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone within your school. Click Deploy.
  5. If Google asks, click Authorize access, pick your school account, and click Allow. Check that this is your own copy and your school account. If school policy blocks approval, ask school IT.
  6. When Google says it is deployed, return to this window and click Find my app link below.

We check that the link belongs to this copy. Finding a link does not test the school’s device or access policy.

Before the first class

  1. Open your teacher dashboard. Use Settings → school directory / enroll by number to connect your school’s private directory and enroll your own classes. For an independent roster, use Add students.
  2. Open the door kiosk on the actual classroom device. Confirm sign-out and return using the protected test and your school’s test identity before student use.

Roster, school access and classroom-device checks are still required. This page does not certify those checks.
