Step 3 of 4

Turn it on

Google needs you to switch it on yourself, once. It is about six clicks.

  1. Open the program ↗
    A new tab opens with the Hall Pass program. You do not need to change anything there.
  2. Top right, click the blue Deploy, then New deployment.
  3. Click the gear ⚙ next to “Select type” and choose Web app.
  4. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone within your school. Click Deploy.
  5. If Google asks, click Authorize access, pick your school account, and click Allow. (If it says the app isn’t verified: Advanced → Go to Hall Pass. It is your own copy.)
