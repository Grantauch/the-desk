SCHOOLWIDE · START HERE
Teacher Setup Preview v0.1.0 — November 2026
=============================================================

STATUS: TRAINING ONLY. NOT STUDENT-FACING. NOT A LIVE INSTALLER.

1. Unzip this package into a folder on your own computer.
2. Double-click START-HERE.html to open the guided local preview.
3. Follow the five steps:
   - What this system will do once approved
   - Door Chromebook versus private teacher computer
   - Existing lunch-number PIN preservation
   - Fictional kiosk session/privacy reset test
   - School-approved release-readiness checklist
4. On the privacy test, choose a fictional 5-digit or 6-digit
   sample. Click "Finish & clear" or switch browser tabs. The
   demonstration must return to "Welcome".
5. Do not enter real student IDs, PINs, email addresses, names,
   teacher credentials, recovery codes, or school secrets.

NO SCHOOLWIDE ACCESS IS ENABLED BY THIS PACKAGE.

The working classroom Hall Pass and Check-In systems remain
authoritative. The existing lunch-number PINs must be preserved,
not bulk reset or exported into a classroom installer.

The standalone page contains no external libraries, images, server
addresses, analytics, storage writes, or network requests. It uses
fictional, local browser-only interaction and cannot submit a pass.

For a real pilot, the rollout team still needs district approval
for student identity/PIN use, verified Google staff/student auth,
two physical devices and campus Wi-Fi testing, historical data
reconciliation, backup/rollback evidence, and monitoring.

Do not put a real kiosk URL on the school network until the
student-facing pilot is explicitly authorized and tested.

The ZIP has PACKAGE-MANIFEST.json with SHA-256 file checksums.
Unzipping and completing the preview is not authorization to
activate any account or student record.
