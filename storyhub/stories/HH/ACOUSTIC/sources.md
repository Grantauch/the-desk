# the cat that wouldn’t spy

An actual CIA experiment; its most famous punch line is disputed.

## S1 · CIA, Natural Spies (2024)

https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/natural-spies-animals-in-espionage/

The agency’s retrospective: 1964–1967, working equipment, limited tests and its rejection of the taxi story. It is also the institution defending its own conduct.

## S2 · Views on Trained Cats (March 1967)

https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB54/

Document 27 is a contemporary, heavily redacted CIA memo. Use its conclusion about practicality; the archive’s accompanying taxi anecdote is a separate later account.

## S3 · Smithsonian interviews (2013)

https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/the-cias-most-highly-trained-spies-werent-even-human-20149/

Interviews report conflicting recollections of the program and the cat’s fate. Useful for comparing testimony, rather than treating one entertaining version as settled.
