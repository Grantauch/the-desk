## Evidence desk addition (2026-10-27)

- USS Swordfish (SSN-579): put into Yokosuka on March 17, 1968 for repairs to a bent periscope. Soviet officials suggested a collision with K-129. The U.S. Navy said the damage came from ice in the Sea of Japan and that Swordfish was about 2,000 nautical miles from K-129. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Swordfish_(SSN-579)
