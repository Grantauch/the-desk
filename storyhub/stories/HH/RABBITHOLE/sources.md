# Down the Rabbit Hole — sources and provenance

## Teacher source

`Down_the_Rabbit_Hole_HH_Mind_Games_L1.pptx`, 19 slides supplied by the lesson author, October 29, 2026. This source controls the sequence, title, four historical case summaries, invented Card E, bias mappings, Wonderland metaphors and final terms. It is not a substitute for independent verification of every secondary claim or number. The four historical cases are Poyais, George C. Parker, Han van Meegeren and Anna Anderson. No student data, hidden keys or private records are published.

## Independent research pointers

- Buckels, Jones and Paulhus (2013), “Behavioral Confirmation of Everyday Sadism,” *Psychological Science*, https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797613490749 . In Study 1, N=71 and 26.8% selected the simulated bug-killing task. It is not a population rate.
- Paulhus and Williams (2002), “The Dark Triad of personality: Narcissism, Machiavellianism, and psychopathy,” *Journal of Research in Personality*, https://www2.psych.ubc.ca/~dpaulhus/research/SELF-ENHANCEMENT/downloads/ARTICLES/JRP.02.pdf .
- American Psychiatric Association, the Goldwater Rule and its limits, https://www.psychiatry.org/news-room/goldwater-rule .
- Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen, *The Supper at Emmaus*, Han van Meegeren (1937), https://www.boijmans.nl/en/collection/artworks/101464/the-men-at-emmaus .
- King’s College London, *The Death Penalty for Forgery?* (Han van Meegeren), https://www.kcl.ac.uk/research/the-death-penalty-for-forgery .
- Lewis Carroll, *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland* (1865) and *Through the Looking-Glass* (1871): character imagery and metaphors, not evidence of contemporary psychological science.

## Evidence boundaries

- Card E (Raymond Kessler, Mileage Plus, Detroit 1958) is intentionally fictional; do not cite as a true case.
- The source presentation's bias-to-case matches are teaching interpretations, not experimentally proven diagnoses of each real participant.
- 137 paintings traded for the forged Vermeer, 180 Poyais deaths and other precise popular retelling figures should be checked against archival evidence before use as exact settled totals.
- The Dark Triad comprises research traits; a classroom scenario or public behavior cannot establish a medical diagnosis.

## Art rights and representation

The fourteen optimized WebP files are direct derivatives of the original PowerPoint's embedded classroom artwork. Cards, spiral eye, checkerboard and Cheshire Cat / Queen of Hearts / Walrus are interpretive illustrated objects; they are not archival photographs or historical likeness evidence. The lesson overlays prose/interaction in HTML, rather than flattening its artwork into screens.
