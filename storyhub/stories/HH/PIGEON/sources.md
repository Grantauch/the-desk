# the pigeon in the nose cone

A real laboratory guidance project. The pigeons were never combat pilots.

## S1 · Smithsonian, the surviving nose cone

https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_690069

Museum object 1981.0997.02, gift of B. F. Skinner. A surviving experimental apparatus establishes development, not combat deployment.

## S2 · B. F. Skinner Foundation, Project Pigeon

https://www.bfskinner.org/project-pigeon/

Includes a silent historical film of training and a link to Skinner’s own retrospective. Film of a trained response is not film of a weapon flying in combat.

## S3 · U.S. Army, It’s a Bird! It’s a Plane! (2013)

https://www.army.mil/article/111511/its_a_bird_its_a_plane

Retrospective history of the project, General Mills support and the later Orcon program. Distinguish demonstrations from operational service.
