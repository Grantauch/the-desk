# the aircraft carrier that needed a freezer

A real secret research project and a floating test model. The giant carrier never entered service.

## S1 · National Research Council Canada, Project Habbakuk

https://www.nrc.canada.ca/en/corporate/history/1916-1945

The participating research institution explains the air-coverage problem, reinforced ice, the prototype and the material, refrigeration and strategic obstacles. Its full-size carrier image is labeled a fantasy illustration.

## S2 · Jasper Museum, the Patricia Lake model

https://jaspermuseum.org/habbakuk-1943-model-ice-sawdust-ship/

Local museum account of the 1943 refrigerated test model and its surviving site. A test structure on a Canadian lake is different from an operational Atlantic carrier.

## S3 · Alberta Aviation Museum, Operation Habbakuk

https://albertaaviationmuseum.com/operation-habbakuk-the-iceberg-aircraft-carrier/

Describes the mid-Atlantic air gap, experimental construction and the engineering and changing wartime requirements. Retellings differ on some demonstration anecdotes; this lesson does not depend on them.
