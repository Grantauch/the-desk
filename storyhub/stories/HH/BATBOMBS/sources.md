# the bats that burned the wrong base

A real secret weapons test that never became a combat mission.

## S1 · U.S. Army historian, Bats Over Tokyo (2008)

https://www.army.mil/article-amp/6993/bats_over_tokyo

Traces the 1942 proposal, development problems, a 1943 accidental fire and the program’s cancellation. A retrospective, not a combat report.

## S2 · National Park Service, Bat Bombs and Balloons on Fire

https://www.nps.gov/articles/batbombsandballoons.htm

Describes the Carlsbad test incident and why animal behavior complicated delivery. The broad scale of the damage is described differently in later retellings.

## S3 · Air Force Test Center history (2021)

https://www.aftc.af.mil/News/On-This-Day-in-Test-History/Article-Display-Test-History/Article/2555256/may-2-1943-a-team-headed-by-dr-lytle-adams-arrived-to-conduct-air-drop-tests-on/

Confirms actual air-drop testing and that the weapon was never used operationally. Consult the historical record; the lesson omits weapon-making specifications.
