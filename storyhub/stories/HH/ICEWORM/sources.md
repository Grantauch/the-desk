# the city that couldn’t hold still

Camp Century was built. The much larger Iceworm missile network was not.

## S1 · NASA, New View of the City Under the Ice (2024)

https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/new-view-of-the-city-under-the-ice-153616/

New radar imagery of the known Camp Century site. It shows a Cold War base, not a newly discovered ancient civilization.

## S2 · Colgan and colleagues, Geophysical Research Letters (2016)

https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/2016GL069688

Primary research distinguishes Camp Century from the unrealized Iceworm concept. Its future waste-exposure discussion uses climate scenarios; it is not a certain countdown.

## S3 · USACE Digital Library, The Story of Camp Century

https://usace.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/collection/p16021coll1/id/34

The Army’s historical promotional film documents construction and daily life. It is useful evidence of the base and of how the Army presented its work.

## S4 · GEUS, Camp Century is staying under the ice (2021)

https://eng.geus.dk/about/news/news-archive/2021/march/camp-century

Later scientific assessment of site conditions. Present-day monitoring and future projections are separate from the 1960s engineering failure.
