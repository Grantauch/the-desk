# the spies who stared at mars

The remote-viewing program was real. Its Martian claims remain unverified.

## S1 · CIA History Staff retrospective (2021)

https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers/

Confirms government remote-viewing research beginning in 1972, transfer among agencies, the 1995 review and the decision not to restore the program. Avoid relying on its simplified chronology of code names.

## S2 · Mars Exploration, May 22, 1984

https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/cia/CIA-RDP96-00788R001900760001-9.pdf

Primary government transcript, CIA-RDP96-00788R001900760001-9, preserved by The Black Vault. The target sheet specifies Mars at approximately one million years B.C. The source records a session, not a verified exploration.

## S3 · AIR, An Evaluation of Remote Viewing (September 29, 1995)

https://documents3.theblackvault.com/documents/stargate/pdfs/CIA-RDP96-00791R000200180006-4.pdf

Primary commissioned evaluation, CIA-RDP96-00791R000200180006-4. Executive summary separates statistical findings, uncertain explanation and poor operational usefulness. Specialist reviewers disagreed about interpretation.
