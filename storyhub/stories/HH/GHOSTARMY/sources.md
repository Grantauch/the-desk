# the army that wasn’t there

Real soldiers staged an army using rubber, sound and radio deception.

## S1 · U.S. Army, Ghost Army deception tactics (2021)

https://www.army.mil/article/247350

Describes the 1,100-person 23rd Headquarters Special Troops and its visual, sonic, radio and impersonation methods. Includes a National Archives photograph of a dummy tank.

## S2 · U.S. Army, Pine Camp sonic-deception history (2020)

https://www.army.mil/article-amp/240486/fort_drum_exhibit_to_highlight_sonic_deception_training_at_pine_camp_during_wwii

Explains recording and playback, January 1944 activation, specialized components and postwar secrecy.

## S3 · U.S. Army Airborne and Special Operations Museum

https://www.asomf.org/the-ghost-army/

Distinguishes the 23rd from the separate 3133rd unit, describes operational risk and museum artifacts. Casualty claims refer to the 23rd.

## S4 · Army video: Ghost Army artists (2014)

https://www.dvidshub.net/video/324277/soldiers-update-ghost-army-artists-part-1

A short official video supplement. Third-party playback can be unavailable on school networks; the complete story remains in the lesson.
