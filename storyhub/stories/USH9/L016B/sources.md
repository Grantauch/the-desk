- [Geary Act text](https://loveman.sdsu.edu/docs/1892GearyAct.pdf), May 5, 1892, 27 Stat. 25. Sections 3, 4, 6 and 7; photograph amendment, November 1893.
- [Yick Wo v. Hopkins](https://www.loc.gov/item/usrep118356/), 118 U.S. 356 (1886).
- [Chae Chan Ping v. United States](https://www.loc.gov/item/usrep130581/), 130 U.S. 581 (1889).
- [Fong Yue Ting v. United States](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/149/698), 149 U.S. 698 (1893).
- [United States v. Wong Kim Ark](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep169/usrep169649/usrep169649.pdf), 169 U.S. 649 (1898).
- [Wong Kim Ark departure statement](https://www.archives.gov/exhibits/documented-rights/exhibit/section2/detail/departure-statement.html), November 5, 1894, National Archives identifier 2641490. The original photograph remains part of the displayed document.
- [Angel Island: By the Numbers](https://www.aiisf.org/vault/bythenumbers), revised detention estimates.
- [National Archives Chinese American records guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/chinese-americans/guide).
- Jean Pfaelzer, *Driven Out* (2007), and the Tacoma Method project for expulsion histories.

The student source panel also identifies the Scott and Magnuson acts, historical speeches, testimony, and an English translation of the Angel Island poem.
