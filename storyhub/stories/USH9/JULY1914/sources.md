## Maps and corresponding source

Country geometry retained from the supplied draft, checked against André Ourednik and contributors, historical-basemaps, world_1914.geojson: https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps/blob/master/geojson/world_1914.geojson . The dataset is a work in progress. Source snapshot and GPL-3.0 text are in source/world_1914.geojson and source/COPYING. Derived maps and this hub's original code are distributed under GPL-3.0; archival images retain their stated rights. The public repository provides corresponding editable SVG, data and JavaScript.

Country order (Luxembourg, Switzerland, Britain, Belgium...) and outlines match the dataset. Maps are continental-scale teaching reconstructions; movement arrows and front lines are schematic. Colors distinguish alignment or involvement, not occupied land. Colonial categories include dominions/protectorates and unequal forms of control; local sovereignty and Indigenous nations are simplified. Area ratios retained from the draft's geographic estimates are rounded for comparison and explicitly labeled approximate, not official territorial statistics.

Videos are optional YouTube embeds supplied in the draft, never downloaded or rehosted; direct links remain available. All essential instruction is also present as text. No claim is made that a video can play through every school filter.

The MAIN mnemonic organizes long-term causes. It is not an exhaustive causal model. The hub distinguishes Serbia's nationalist conspirators from proven authorization by the Serbian government, occupation in 1878 from annexation in 1908, mobilization from declaration, and alliances from automatic war.
