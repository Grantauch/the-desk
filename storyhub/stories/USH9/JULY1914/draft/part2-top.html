Sarajevo to London · June 28 to August 4, 1914

Thirty Seven Days

On a Sunday morning in Sarajevo, a 19 year old student fired two shots into an open car. Thirty seven days later, Britain declared war on Germany and most of Europe was fighting. The clearest way to see how that happened is on a map.

Map 1 · Europe in June 1914

Two sides already drawn

Germany and Austria Hungary signed an alliance in 1879, and Italy joined them in 1882 to make the Triple Alliance. France and Russia answered with their own alliance in 1894. Britain settled old quarrels with France in 1904 and with Russia in 1907, and those three became the Triple Entente. Pick any country to see who it had promised to stand beside.

Map 2 · The world in 1914

Small countries, giant empires

The same powers that faced off in Europe ruled much of the rest of the planet. By 1914 Europeans controlled about 90 percent of Africa, and only Ethiopia and Liberia stayed independent. Rivalry over colonies brought Germany and France close to war over Morocco in 1905 and again in 1911. Pick an empire to light up everything it ruled.
