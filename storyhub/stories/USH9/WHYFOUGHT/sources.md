# Why They Fought: sources

- Christopher Clark, The Sleepwalkers, 2012, for the July Crisis decisions and the blank check of July 5 and 6, 1914.
- Margaret MacMillan, The War That Ended Peace, 2013, for the naval race, Alsace and Lorraine and the Ottoman battleships.
- Paul Kennedy, The Rise and Fall of the Great Powers, 1987, for 1913 population and steel.
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, World War I casualty table, for forces mobilized and military deaths.
- Willy and Nicky telegram of July 29, 1914; Bethmann Hollweg's scrap of paper remark reported by Sir Edward Goschen; Bülow's place in the sun speech, December 1897; Wilson's address of April 2, 1917.
- Imperial War Museums, How did WW1 Start?, YouTube c7nUDLKKEBY, embedded.
