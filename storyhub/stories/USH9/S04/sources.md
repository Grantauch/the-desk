# the weaver's son

Standalone hub on the Luddites and what happened to the workers the machines replaced.

## Core factual sources

- Daron Acemoglu and Simon Johnson, Learning from Ricardo and Thompson, MIT, 2024, and the MIT Stone Center research summary.
- Robert C. Allen, The Hand Loom Weaver and the Power Loom, 2017.
- Hansard, House of Commons debates on handloom weavers, June 11, 1834, and July 28, 1835.
- Mark Hovell, The Chartist Movement, 1918.
- E. P. Thompson, The Making of the English Working Class, 1963.
- Kevin Binfield, Writings of the Luddites, and the Luddite history at Murray State University. Adrian Randall on the Luddite letters.
- Lord Byron, House of Lords speech, February 27, 1812. David Ricardo, Principles of Political Economy and Taxation, third edition, 1821.
- Luddite Bicentenary and Lancaster Castle records for Middleton, Westhoughton and the Lancaster executions.
- PwC Global AI Jobs Barometer, 2026. Kosmyna and others, Your Brain on ChatGPT, MIT Media Lab, 2025.

## Accuracy / interpretation notes

- The three and a half to one claim is labeled as a claim that spread, not a measured experiment.
- The 1830s wage figures come from testimony to Parliament and vary by town and cloth.
- Abraham Charlson's age is listed as 16 in contemporary records.
- The MIT study is small and was not peer reviewed when released.
