# THE LONG FUSE — Source and Rights Register

Historical teaching source is the 40-slide The Long Fuse Batch24 master, compiled from primary materials and validated in Batches 18–24. This Story Hub is a **new interpretation**, not a transcript of every slide.

## Image provenance

A contemporary dramatization by an artist, NOT a photo or direct eyewitness depiction; cropped/converted from an existing public Grant Desk asset.
- **German troops in Belgium, 1914.** Bain News Service / Library of Congress, [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Germans_in_Belgium_-_on_the_way_to_the_front_LCCN2014703590.jpg). Public domain. Not necessarily August 4.

## Research anchors
- [July 1914 Austrian ultimatum](https://www.firstworldwar.com/source/austrianultimatum.htm) — teacher paraphrase, not a forged facsimile
- [Serbia's response to the ultimatum](https://www.firstworldwar.com/source/serbianreply.htm) — compared for disputed judicial inquiry participation
- [Treaty of Versailles, Article 231](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/imt/partviii.asp) — legal liability wording, not a simple confession of sole guilt
- [U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum: Hitler comes to power](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/hitler-comes-to-power) — distinction between Nazi electoral support and appointment.
- [Bundesarchiv image source catalog](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/) — original archive institution.

## Exclusions
The potentially disputed IWM-use photographs of Somme and Armistice Day, and the Topical Press/Getty 1922 portrait, are **not included** in this new public-facing Story Hub. No false documentary facsimiles, fabricated voices or invented quotes. This register is a production review, not a legal opinion.
