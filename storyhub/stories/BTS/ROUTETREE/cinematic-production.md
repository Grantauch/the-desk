## Scope and baseline

The live baseline uses fourteen strongly recolored contextual place photographs, illustrated SVGs and YouTube facades. The ten NFL stories already have useful route filtering, probability visualization, movie claim sorting, draft comparisons and personal writing. Those interactions and their answer fields are preserved. PR #406 adds twelve photographic slots; this edition is stacked on that branch and fills the ten NFL slots with researched photographs. Ayres and Morris retain their existing footage and contextual images.

The pilot adds four finished raster scenes: the opening stadium interpretation, Papale at camp, Warner stocking shelves and Bleier with the four rings. Seven further NFL stories gain authentic photographic anchors, dated to avoid implying that a later portrait records an earlier turning point. They retain their existing contextual illustrations and videos; seven additional large reconstructed scenes are not claimed as finished.

## Historical and rights decisions

- Papale's practice scene is a labeled reconstruction of Widener camp, not an authenticated exchange, exact campus view or Veterans Stadium workout. The real later portrait remains separate. The player's rear view avoids claiming an authenticated young face.
- Warner's store is reconstructed from the documented job, with the real 2007 portrait used only as a likeness reference. His age, clothing, pose and store interior are interpreted. John Trainor and CC BY 2.0 are credited.
- Bleier's 2009 conference photograph is the documentary source for the two men and rings, not evidence of his 1969 hospitalization. Surface details in the overpainting are interpretive.
- NASA's aerial is from 2002 in baseball configuration. The illustration is not a measured map; generated small details may differ.
- No movie still, Getty/AP photo or invented newspaper clipping is newly distributed. Existing recreated receipts and postcard retain their existing educational roles.
- Every new photograph has a source record, date, creator, reuse basis and original checksum in `assets/ci-rights.json`. CC BY-SA adaptations retain their license. Lane's newspaper source has a US public-domain basis; international commercial redistribution needs separate territorial review. Photographic copyright permission is not an endorsement or an advertising/merchandise clearance.

## Final production prompt set

Built-in image production was used, with the owner-supplied final player/coach image as a style reference. Its file is not redistributed in the repository. Four production briefs were used:

1. **Papale / historical-scene:** documentary eye-level 3:2 illustration of a number83 player in three-quarter rear view listening to a coach pointing at a play sheet, Widener College training camp in summer1976. White mesh practice uniform, green windbreaker, believable 1970s equipment and normal daylight. A reconstructed human exchange; no invented recognizable player face, giant stadium, movie actor, dramatic sky, orange glow or poster pose. Dimensional ink, selective crosshatching and localized fine halftone.
2. **Opening / style-transfer:** reinterpret NASA's 2002 Veterans Stadium overhead source with architectural ink contours, natural green, concrete ivory and asphalt charcoal. Preserve the general bowl, parking, roads and baseball configuration. No clouds, people, skyline, route graphics, labels or fake archival grain. Generated detail is explicitly interpretive.
3. **Warner / historical-scene:** 3:2 candid eye-level night-shift grocery stocking scene, Cedar Falls circa1994, age23. John Trainor's 2007 portrait supplies only a de-aged facial reference; pose, polo, trolley, cardboard and generic 1990s shelving are reconstructed. Directed gaze toward the can, credible hands, cool fluorescent light, dimensional Cinematic Ink. No football props, readable brands, movie actor, heroic pose or vintage filter.
4. **Bleier / identity-preserve and style-transfer:** 3:2 overpainting of Bill Prokopyk's January24,2009 photograph. Preserve Bleier at left, Larsen at right, their expressions, suit, digital camouflage, supporting wrist gesture and four rings. Fine expressive contours, natural faces, selective cloth hatching, restrained halftone and practical indoor light. No added people, wartime setting, stadium, captions or fake grain.

## Review and validation

Full-resolution art inspection checked plausible anatomy, directed attention, four ring-bearing fingers, natural atmosphere, source dates and consistent ink treatment. All generated surfaces and unverified scene details remain labeled interpretations. The source photographs govern factual inference.

Responsive WebP derivatives use 640, 1024 and1536px widths with native dimensions and lazy loading below the opening. Native range controls provide keyboard/touch source reveals and percentage descriptions. Native disclosures expose photographs without JavaScript. No extra student storage, analytics, service, or autoplay is introduced.

The dedicated `Route Tree art review` workflow runs source/asset validation, desktop and mobile keyboard journeys, original-interaction regression checks, full-page WCAG checks, reduced motion, and before/after screenshots. Local Chromium startup is blocked by the workspace's Unix-socket restriction; the browser evidence must come from CI. Canonical local verification, CI, art review, merge, deployment and school-device acceptance remain separate evidence stages.
