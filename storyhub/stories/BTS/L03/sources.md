# BTS L03 — Not In The Room — public source notes

This StoryHub reads early professional baseball rules as business documents. It distinguishes a documented meeting from a later locked-door story and avoids unsupported exact figures where accounts disagree.

## Primary and period sources

- *Constitution of the National League of Professional Base Ball Clubs* (1876), for membership, city size, exclusive territory, common rules, and the declared championship.
- Period scored programs and *Sporting Life* advertising from the 1880s and 1893, for the program, subscription, and measured-audience material.

## Institutional and research sources

- Society for American Baseball Research material on William Hulbert, the National League founding meeting, enforcement, and the early reserve agreement.
- National Baseball Hall of Fame material on Hulbert's league presidency and club expulsions.
- Library of Congress material on box scores and the sporting press.

## Boundaries and disagreements

- The February 2, 1876 meeting is documented. The locked-door detail is identified onscreen as later memoir.
- Accounts compress the Cincinnati expulsion sequence across 1879 and 1880. The student page does not claim one exact year.
- The 1879 reserve agreement began with a limited number of players per club and hardened later. The page does not present the first agreement as the finished lifetime system.
- Salaries varied widely. The page says many players earned more than most industrial workers and does not manufacture a single comparison figure.
- Native HTML diagrams and placeholders are explanatory graphics, not archival evidence. No fake document text or invented historical likeness is presented as a source.
