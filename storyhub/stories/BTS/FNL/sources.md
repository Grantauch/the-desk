# Who Pays for Friday Night?: sources

## Core factual sources

- The book and its reception: H.G. Bissinger, Friday Night Lights: A Town, a Team, and a Dream (Addison Wesley, 1990). Education Week review, January 1991, for the 1988 budget figures. Education Week, October 3, 1990, for the canceled Odessa book signing. Deseret News, 2004, for the T shirts. NPR Fresh Air, August 2015, for sales of about 2 million copies. CBS 60 Minutes Sports, 2015.
- The season: UIL playoff archive for 1988 to 1989. Associated Press, December 11, 1988, for the 14 to 9 semifinal and attendance. MojoLand, Odessa American, Sports Illustrated and LitCharts summaries for the Midland Lee game and the coin toss.
- Boobie Miles: Bissinger's book and his 2012 follow up, After Friday Night Lights, excerpted by Grantland. Houston Chronicle, 2004, on what the film changed.
- Oil: annual average domestic crude prices, 1980 to 1990, as compiled by InflationData from U.S. Energy Information Administration figures. OPEC monthly prices for 1985 and 1986 from Oil and Gas 360. StateImpact Texas, NPR, on Midland and Odessa in the 1980s bust.
- Ratliff Stadium: Ector County ISD figures as reported by MaxPreps, ESPN and Wikipedia.
- Permian titles: UIL records via Texas Football and MojoLand.
- No pass, no play: Texas Tribune, Texas Public Radio and Houston Chronicle reporting on House Bill 72 (1984).
- Dallas Carter: The Battalion (Texas A&M) wire stories of December 9, 1988 and November 8, 1990. Dallas Morning News series, November 2008, republished 2015. ESPN 30 for 30, What Carter Lost (2017).
- The film: Variety and Austin Chronicle reviews, TCM, the Houston Chronicle fact check, and Pitt News coverage of Bissinger's comments on the ending.

## Accuracy / interpretation notes

- The jet figure is approximate in the source. The page says about $70,000.
- Some summaries date the semifinal December 17. The Associated Press report and the UIL archive place it on Saturday, December 10, 1988. December 17 was the final.
- The midnight coin toss followed the Friday, November 4 games, so it is dated November 5, 1988.
- The no pass, no play suspension length changed after 1984. The page describes the rule as first written.
