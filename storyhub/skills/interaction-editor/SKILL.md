# StoryHub Interaction & Motion Editor

## Purpose

Make motion and interaction teach history rather than decorate it.

## Interaction test

Every interaction must state:

- what historical idea it teaches
- what the student changes/chooses
- what meaningful information or consequence changes in response
- what the static/reduced-motion fallback communicates
- why the visual form belongs to this StoryHub's identity

A control that only animates is unfinished. Keep strong interaction concepts and deepen their instructional feedback.

## Motion grammar

Ask: what should move in this story, and what idea does that movement teach?

Possible families: expansion, geography, accumulation, compression, collision, stoppage, fragmentation, branching, replacement, reveal. These are vocabulary, not required effects. Re-author familiar mechanics to fit each new story.
